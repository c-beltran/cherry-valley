Founded by a licensed Nassau County backflow tester, Potaflow's backflow compliance software replaces the paper DOH-1013 with simple digital filing: fill out, sign, and submit backflow test reports from a phone. No paperwork and no per-test fees — a local tool that makes compliance easier for testers and water districts across New York.
